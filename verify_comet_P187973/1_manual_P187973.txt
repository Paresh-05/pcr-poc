## DTC P187873 or P187973

## Diagnostic Description

 Be sure to perform the Diagnostic
Circuit/System Inspection - Vehicle
prior to using the diagnostic
procedure.
 Refer to Strategy-based Diagnosis
for the overview of diagnostic
method.
 The introduction for various
diagnosis categories is provided in
Diagnostic Description.

## DTC Description

DTC P187873：Main switch: main
positive switch stick
DTC P187973：Main switch: main
negative switch stick

## System Description

Hybrid/EV Battery Pack monitors the
cell temp sensor.

## DTC Operating Conditions

 Start 100ms after off.

## DTC Setting Conditions

 DTC P187873：BMS does not
close the precharge relay and main
positive relay, and the detected total
voltage is ≥ 95% of total battery
voltage and the total battery voltage
is ≥ 30V.
 DTC P187973：BMS does not
close the main negative relay, the
detected total voltage is ≥ 95% of
total battery voltage and the total
battery voltage is ≥ 30V.

## Strategy After DTC Appears

 Record the DTC.

## Clear Fault Information Conditions

 After 40 non-fault operating cycles
(from ignition on to ignition off), the
historical DTCs shall be clarified.
 ECU receives a command of
clearing DTCs from the tester.
